============================================================
1. PROJECT VISION
============================================================

Build a Hyperlocal Product Discovery Platform.

Core idea:

"Search any product. Instantly know which nearby shop has it,
at what price, whether it is available, how far it is,
and how to reach the shop."

The platform is primarily a DISCOVERY platform.

The customer discovers products and nearby businesses
and then visits the physical shop/business offline.

This is NOT primarily a home-delivery platform.

Customer flow:

Customer
    ↓
Search Product
    ↓
System understands product
    ↓
Find nearby businesses
    ↓
Compare:
    - availability
    - price
    - distance
    - rating
    - freshness
    ↓
Select Business
    ↓
Get Directions
    ↓
Visit Business Offline
    ↓
Purchase Offline
