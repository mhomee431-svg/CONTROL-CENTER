14. ACCESS TOKEN
============================================================

Access token should be short-lived.

Example:

Do not hardcode secrets.

============================================================
15. REFRESH TOKEN
============================================================

Implement refresh-token based session renewal.

Endpoint:

POST /auth/refresh

Refresh token must be handled securely.

Prefer server-side revocation capability.

Do not make access tokens unnecessarily long-lived.

============================================================
16. LOGOUT
============================================================

Endpoint:

POST /auth/logout

Logout must invalidate/revoke the relevant refresh session.

Client must also remove local authentication state.

Do not return password hashes through APIs.

22. BUSINESS VERIFICATION
============================================================

Authentication and business verification are different.

Login means:

"This user is authenticated."

Verification means:

"This business is approved/verified."

Do not combine them into one flag.
